# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two audiences with different contexts but the same need: confidence that this team delivers cinematic quality worth paying for.

**Small business owners** (Detroit local: restaurants, retailers, event organizers) scrolling casually on their phone, often in the evening, comparing options quickly. They need to see work that looks like their business elevated, not a generic template.

**Marketing decision-makers** (directors, brand managers) comparing videography vendors deliberately at their desk, evaluating craft and professionalism. They need to see strategic thinking behind the visuals, not just pretty footage.

Both arrive unsure whether professional video is worth the investment. Both leave certain it is.

## Product Purpose

Portfolio and conversion site for Gridmark Media, a Detroit videography studio producing strategic social media video content for retail, restaurants, and events. The site exists to: (1) prove craft through the work itself, (2) make pricing transparent and approachable, (3) drive phone calls. Success = a visitor watches the reel, trusts the team, and calls (313) 742-5658.

## Brand Personality

Sleek, assured, tasteful. The voice of someone who knows their craft so well they don't need to sell it hard. Confident without arrogance. Polished without pretension. Detroit-rooted but not performing grit.

## References

Cinema-studio aesthetic: A24, Arri, cinema production house sites. The work is presented like cinema, not commerce. Large-format imagery, generous negative space, restrained typography, the reel as centerpiece. The site itself feels like a screening room.

## Anti-references

- Cookie-cutter freelancer portfolios: stock vibes, template layouts, personality-by-default
- Bloated agency sites: heavy animation, slow load times, spectacle over substance
- Neon club flyer aesthetics: garish colors, flashy effects, style without restraint

## Design Principles

1. **Show, don't tell.** The video work is the proof. The site's job is to get out of the way and let the portfolio speak.
2. **Cinema, not marketing.** Present the portfolio like a cinema house presents its work, not a freelancer hawking services. Screening room, not storefront.
3. **Quiet authority.** Trust comes from precision and restraint, not volume. Every element is deliberate; nothing screams.
4. **Expert confidence.** No need to shout discounts or flash badges. The craft speaks through composition, pacing, and selection.
5. **Detroit-rooted craft.** Grounded in real businesses with real results. Not performing grit, not aspiring to coastal polish. Authentic local expertise.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Best effort. Reasonable accommodations for reduced motion and keyboard navigation. No formal WCAG target, but avoid excluding users through poor contrast, missing focus states, or motion-only information.
